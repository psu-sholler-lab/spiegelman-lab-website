Welcome to the Spiegelman Lab

Our Research Meet the Team

Our lab is focused on the role of RNA-binding proteins in tumor progression. We combine molecular and cell biology, biochemistry, in vivo modeling, and drug design to identify novel molecular targets and evaluate their therapeutic potential across pediatric and adult cancers.

Lab community

Our Team

Our laboratory includes scientists and trainees studying the molecular drivers of cancer, from RNA-binding proteins to metastasis, in the Department of Pediatrics at Penn State College of Medicine.
